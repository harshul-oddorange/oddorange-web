4. How projects run

Reels: we review your footage and brief, prepare an editing plan or storyboard when useful, get your approval, complete the edit, share a review version, then make the included revisions.

SaaS and launch videos: brief and script, sketch storyboard (you approve), visual design and style frames (you approve), motion graphics, then revisions and delivery. Changes to a storyboard or design after you've approved it are quoted separately.

Your approval of a stage, or of the final review version, means that stage is accepted.

5. Revisions

Each package includes the number of revision rounds shown on our pricing and in your quote. A revision is a change within the agreed scope, sent as one consolidated list. New ideas, new footage or changes to approved stages are extra work and will be quoted before we do them. We don't offer unlimited revisions.

6. Payment

Reels: 50% upfront to begin work. The remaining 50% is due after the edit is complete and before we deliver the final, unwatermarked file.

SaaS and launch videos: 50% upfront before work begins. The remaining 50% is due after you approve the visual designs and before animation starts.

Review versions may carry a watermark and must not be published. Final files are delivered once payment is complete. Prices are in Indian Rupees unless your quote is in another currency; any bank, transfer or currency fees on your side are yours.
