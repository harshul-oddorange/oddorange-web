Short version: this website doesn't use cookies.

1. What we use

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts run when a page loads. Fonts and images come from our own website.

The site uses your browser's session storage to remember, for the current visit only, that you've already seen the opening animation, so it doesn't replay on every page. It's deleted when you close the tab, can't identify you and is never shared.

2. YouTube videos

Our videos come from YouTube, but nothing loads until you press play. Once you do, YouTube may set its own cookies or similar storage on your device, under Google's cookie policy.

3. Why there's no cookie banner

Because we only use storage that's strictly needed for the site to work as you'd expect, and nothing that tracks you, there's nothing to ask your consent for. If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll ask first.
